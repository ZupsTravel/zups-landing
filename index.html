How Zups Works

Built for people who'd
rather be traveling.

📮

Postcards, Not Posts

Every place you visit becomes a postcard. Yours in color, the rest of the world in black & white waiting to be unlocked.

🤙

Go With Your People

Share trip proposals with your travel circle, get a quick vibe check, then book together in one tap. No Venmo chaos, no "wait who booked what" — just the same hostel room, locked in.

💎

Hidden Gems, Earned

After you've been somewhere, unlock the ability to leave a hidden gem — human-only, vague by design. A direction, not a map. No rankings, no coordinates. Just a nudge from someone who's been there.
