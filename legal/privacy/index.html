Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026 · Last updated: September 3, 2026

This policy describes what Zups collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It reflects how the app actually works at V1 launch. If something changes materially, we'll update this page and note it here.

Zups is operated by James Zupa ("Zups", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy can go to jameszupa@zupstravel.com.

Summary

  • Zups is a travel planning app. We collect the information needed to run it — nothing more.
  • We do not collect your precise device location (GPS). Ever. Zups uses a manually chosen home airport, not your phone's location.
  • Zups does not ship third-party advertising SDKs, does not display third-party behavioral advertisements in the app, and does not use third-party analytics vendors, crash reporters, or session replay tools.
  • Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method. Apple may relay a randomized email to us — that's your choice, not ours.
  • At V1 launch, all Zups accounts are private-by-default. Your postcards and profile are not visible to other Zups users.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This is a real, hard delete.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Apple identifier and, if you allow it, the email address Apple provides on first sign-in (which may be Apple's private relay address). On your first sign-in only, Apple may also share your name.
  • Profile details you enter in the app: username, bio, profile photo, home city, home airport (a 3-letter airport code you choose from a list), number of travelers, and passport country (optional).

Content you create

  • Trips you plan (destinations, dates, saved templates, notes).
  • Postcards you create (photo you upload, caption, back-of-postcard text, template selection).
  • Journal entries you write inside the app.
  • Saved trips, saved trip collections, and any community memberships you join.

Photos and media

If you upload a photo (for your profile or a postcard), the photo is stored on our servers so we can display it back to you. We do not scan the contents of your photo, extract metadata, or share it with any third party.

Home base — no GPS

Zups needs to know your "home airport" to plan trips from it. You choose this manually — you type a city name, we look it up against OpenStreetMap's public geocoder to find the nearest airports, and you pick one. We store the city name and 3-letter airport code. We do not store latitude/longitude, and we never ask your device for your GPS location. The location permission dialog does not appear in Zups V1.

How you use the app (product analytics)

Zups records first-party product-interaction events — for example, opening a trip idea, saving a trip, starting to plan, or tapping a booking link. This helps us understand which content is useful and which is not. These events are stored in our own database (Supabase) linked to your account. We do not send them to Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, Google Analytics, Segment, Facebook, or any other analytics vendor. There is no third-party analytics SDK in the app.

Booking handoff records

When you tap a "book with" link inside Zups, we log the fact that you clicked it — including origin, destination, and dates — so we can understand what people are trying to book. If a partner completes a booking, they may send us a delayed confirmation (see "Booking partners" below). No credit card, passport, or personal booking detail is ever sent through Zups — you complete the booking on the partner's website.

Support communications

If you email us, we keep the email so we can respond.

What we don't collect at V1 launch

  • Precise device location (GPS).
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera roll access (beyond a photo you deliberately select for upload).
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Cross-app tracking data.
  • Health, financial, or biometric information.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the app: display your trips, postcards, journal, and profile back to you.
  • To sign you in (via Apple) and keep your session active.
  • To send booking-partner links pre-filled with your trip context (origin, destination, dates) so the partner's search page opens with the right query.
  • To understand which product features are useful, in aggregate, from your first-party interaction events.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with law when we're legally required to.

We do not use your information for advertising, retargeting, profiling, or sale to data brokers.

3. Location — clear statement

Zups does not obtain your precise location from your device. The iOS Location permission prompt does not appear in Zups V1. The only "location" data associated with your account is the home city label and 3-letter airport code you chose during onboarding. You can change this in Settings at any time.

Booking partner data — what actually gets sent

When you tap a "book flights", "find hotels", "browse experiences" or similar link, Zups opens the partner's website with a URL that includes: the trip context (origin, destination, dates, adult count) and our affiliate marker. Zups also includes a random identifier used solely so the partner can tell us if a booking eventually completes. This random identifier is not your Zups account ID or email — it's a separate opaque token stored server-side. We do not send your name, email, Apple identifier, or precise location to booking partners.

Once you land on the partner's website, the partner's own privacy policy governs what happens next — including any cookies they set, any account you create with them, and any booking you make.

5. Sign in with Apple

Zups uses Sign in with Apple as the only way to create or sign into an account. Apple gives us an opaque identifier for you, an email address (which may be Apple's private relay), and — on your first sign-in only — your name if you chose to share it. We store these to represent your account. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" relay, we cannot see your real email address.

When you delete your Zups account (see below), we attempt to revoke Apple's authorization so the link between your Apple ID and your (now-deleted) Zups account is severed. If you later sign into Zups with the same Apple ID, Apple treats it as a brand-new user and you get a fresh, empty account.

6. Postcard and profile visibility (V1)

At V1 launch, every Zups account is private. This is enforced at the database level, not just in the app UI:

  • Your postcards, saved trips, journal entries, and follows are visible only to you.
  • Other users cannot search for you, view your profile, or see your postcards.
  • Communities (topic groups seeded by Zups — e.g. Solo Travelers, Europe, Budget Travel) are the one shared surface. If you post a postcard to a Community you've joined, other members of that Community can see it. Community postcards are still served through signed, time-limited URLs — they aren't publicly indexable.
  • The Zups app UI does not currently offer a switch to make your account public. This may change in a future release; if it does, we'll update this page before any change takes effect.

7. User-generated content, reporting, and moderation

You control the content you create. Zups V1 supports reporting and blocking so other members of a Community can flag content they find objectionable:

  • Any user can report a postcard shared to a Community. Reports go into our system for review.
  • If three or more distinct users report the same postcard, it is automatically hidden from public view pending review.
  • Users can also block other users; blocked users cannot interact with your content.
  • We also apply a server-side content filter that rejects obviously abusive text in postcards before it ever becomes visible.
  • We review reports and respond to launch-blocking abuse within a reasonable time. For urgent safety concerns, email jameszupa@zupstravel.com.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your Zups account from inside the app:

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account → confirm twice.

When you delete your account, we:

  • Delete your profile row and your Supabase Authentication user.
  • Delete every trip, postcard, journal entry, saved trip, follow, group membership, block, and report you created, across the 27 tables that reference your account.
  • Delete every file you uploaded to Zups storage — profile photo, postcard photos, trip images, trip videos.
  • Attempt to revoke Apple's authorization so Sign in with Apple later creates a fresh account instead of resurrecting the old one.

What we retain after deletion, and why: we keep the aggregate booking-handoff and booking-link-click rows that record which trips led to which partner clicks. Before we retain them, we remove your account association from those rows — they no longer contain your Zups account identifier. We keep them because they're how we understand which trip ideas convert into booking attempts, in aggregate, and how we're paid by our booking partners. To be precise: we do not describe these rows as "anonymous"; they are retained in a form no longer associated with your Zups account, and they still contain the trip-context fields already in the row (origin, destination, dates, partner).

Supabase's own authentication server logs (records that a login happened, tokens issued, etc.) are retained by Supabase according to their retention policy and are not directly controllable by us.

If you'd rather delete your account by email — for example if you can't get into the app — write to jameszupa@zupstravel.com from the email associated with your account (or from your Apple-relay email) and we'll process it manually.

9. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete it as described above. If you become inactive but don't delete your account, we keep your data so it's ready when you come back.

Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, then discarded.

10. Data location

Zups' primary backend (Supabase) currently runs in the United States. If you use Zups from outside the US, you're consenting to your data being processed there. Apple, Expo, and our booking partners operate globally.

11. Minimum age

Zups is not intended for anyone under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has created a Zups account, email us and we will delete it.
