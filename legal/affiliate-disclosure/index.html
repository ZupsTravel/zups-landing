Affiliate Disclosure

A plain-language explanation of how Zups earns money.

Zups is free to use. When you're ready to book — a flight, a hotel, a hostel, a ferry, a tour or experience, ground transport — Zups hands you off to a booking partner's website to complete the reservation.

Some of these outbound links are affiliate links. If you book something through them, Zups may receive a small commission from the partner — at no additional cost to you. Your price is the same whether you clicked through Zups or went to the partner's website directly.

Who our booking partners are

These are the partners Zups links out to at V1 launch:

  • Flights: Travelpayouts / Kiwi.com
  • Accommodations: Expedia Group's Travel Creator Program, Hostelworld
  • Ground transport: Omio, Rome2Rio, and — for a small number of island routes — direct ferry-operator websites (for example, Caremar, NLG, Blue Star, Jadrolinija)
  • Experiences and tours: GetYourGuide, Viator

Zups is not the seller. The partner is. When you book, you're booking with the partner under their terms and their privacy policy.

How the link works, technically

When you tap a "book with" link in Zups, we open the partner's website with a URL that includes:

  • Your trip parameters — origin, destination, dates, adult count — so the partner's search page opens with the right query.
  • Zups' affiliate marker for that partner, so the partner knows the click came from us.
  • A random identifier used solely so the partner can tell us — separately, later — if a booking eventually completes. That random identifier is not your Zups account ID, email, or Apple identifier.

We do not send your name, email, Apple identifier, precise location, payment info, or booking details to any partner. Those never leave your device (or are entered directly on the partner's site, not through Zups). See our Privacy Policy for the full data-flow.

Does this affect what Zups shows me?

Trip ideas, destination content, and route options in Zups are chosen based on what we think is genuinely useful — not based on which partner pays more. Where we can display multiple options for the same segment, we try to show what's actually cheapest or fastest. If we ever change this, we'll say so here.

In-app disclosure

This page explains the affiliate relationship as a whole. In addition, the Zups app itself notes near booking hand-off points that some outbound links are affiliate links.

Not investment or booking advice

Anything Zups shows about prices, routes, or destinations is informational. Verify important details on the partner's site or the destination's official sources before you commit money to a trip. See our Terms of Service for more.
